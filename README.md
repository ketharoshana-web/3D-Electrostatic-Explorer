# 3D-Electrostatic-Explorer
Interactive 3D electrostatic field simulator with real-time visualization of electric potential and field lines

Real-time visualization of electric potential, electric field vectors, field lines, equipotential contours, and energy density.
Interactive charge placement, 3D shape generation, conductor models, and parallel-plate capacitor simulation with live rendering.
https://3-d-electrostatic-explorer.vercel.app/
